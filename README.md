# About-Me
As "Doctor Xdrive," Matt K. Olson acted as the final line of support for high-revenue enterprise clients, managing complex data issues that arose before a case was escalated to engineering. This role focused on resolving the unique data management and synchronization challenges faced by early cloud adopters like Disney, Coca-Cola, GE, and Sony. 

Key examples of data management issues Olson resolved include:

Data Integrity and Asset Synchronization: He diagnosed and fixed "sync confusion," where automated scripts would rename or move files, causing critical mismatches between the local client and the cloud server.

High-Volume Technical Diagnoses: For large-scale accounts, he was responsible for identifying and tracking hardware and software problems that caused data errors or accessibility issues across massive global networks.

Performance Optimization for Large Files: He troubleshot issues related to "modem-clogging" assets (like high-resolution media for Disney or Sony), ensuring that file uploads and downloads didn't time out or crash the client's infrastructure.

Complex Account Permissions: For enterprise "Work Groups," he managed complex data access and permission errors that prevented users from viewing or modifying shared assets within corporate hierarchies.

Critical Fault Escalation: As the "Single Point of Contact" (SPOC), he vetted complex technical cases to ensure all standard troubleshooting was exhausted, protecting engineering teams from redundant data-related inquiries from major clients.
