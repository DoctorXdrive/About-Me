# About-Me
As "Doctor Xdrive," Matt K. Olson acted as the final line of support for high-revenue enterprise clients, managing complex data issues that arose before a case was escalated to engineering. This role focused on resolving the unique data management and synchronization challenges faced by early cloud adopters like <b>Disney</b>, <b>Coca-Cola</b>, <b>GE</b>, <b>Sony</b>, and many others, to many to mention here. 

<b>Key examples of data management issues Matt K. Olson resolved include:</b>

<b>Data Integrity and Asset Synchronization:</b> Matt diagnosed and fixed "sync confusion," where automated scripts would rename or move files, causing critical mismatches between the local client and the cloud server.

<b>High-Volume Technical Diagnoses:</b> For large-scale accounts, Matt was responsible for identifying and tracking hardware and software problems that caused data errors or accessibility issues across massive global networks.

<b>Performance Optimization for Large Files:</b> Matt troubleshot issues related to "modem-clogging" assets (like high-resolution media for <b>Disney</b>, <b>GE</b>, or <b>Sony</b>), ensuring that file uploads and downloads didn't time out or crash the client's infrastructure.

<b>Complex Account Permissions:</b> For enterprise "Work Groups," Matt managed complex data access and permission errors that prevented users from viewing or modifying shared assets within corporate hierarchies.

<b>Critical Fault Escalation:</b> As the "Single Point of Contact" (SPOC), Matt vetted complex technical cases to ensure all high-level troubleshooting was exhausted, protecting engineering teams from redundant data-related inquiries from major clients.
